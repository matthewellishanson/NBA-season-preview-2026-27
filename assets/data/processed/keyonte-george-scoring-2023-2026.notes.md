# Keyonte George scoring chart data

Generated by `scripts/prepare_jazz_charts.py` from the supplied Basketball Reference-style regular-season tables:

- `KeyonteGeorgePerGame.csv` (per-game minutes, points and assists)
- `KeyonteGeorgeP100P.csv` (total minutes, points and assists per 100 possessions)
- `KeyonteGeorgeAdv.csv` (true shooting and usage)

Rows are joined only when season, team, age, league, position, games and starts agree. Coverage is limited to 2023-24 through 2025-26; the incomplete 2026-27 rows are excluded. `TS%` remains a fraction while `USG%` remains in percentage units. The script separately validates per-game and total-minute meanings.
