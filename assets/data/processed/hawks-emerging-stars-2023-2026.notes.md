# Atlanta emerging-stars chart data

`hawks-emerging-stars-2023-2026.csv` is generated by running
`python scripts/prepare_hawks_emerging_stars.py` from the repository root. The
six supplied Basketball Reference-style source exports in `assets/data/raw/`
remain unchanged.

The output contains one row per player and completed regular season for
2023-24, 2024-25 and 2025-26. The exports are player-specific and do not
include names or IDs, so the script supplies stable player identities and
joins Per Game, Per 100 Possessions and Advanced rows by player, season and
the shared team/aggregate identifier. Age, league, position, games and starts
must also agree. Career summaries, repeated headers, seasons outside the
requested window and any incomplete future season are excluded.

If a future export contains a combined `TOT`, `2TM` or other `nTM` row plus
individual team stints, the combined row is selected and the stints are
excluded. Multiple stints without a combined row fail preparation. Rates and
percentages are never summed or averaged across stints.

Points and assists per 100 possessions are retained directly from the Per 100
tables. True shooting percentage is retained directly from the Advanced table.
Games and minutes per game come from the Per Game tables; total minutes come
from Per 100 and must agree with Advanced. Source row IDs are included for
traceability. The chart uses Johnson's points and assists per 100, and
Alexander-Walker's points per 100 and TS%; the additional normalized fields
support validation and accessible context.
