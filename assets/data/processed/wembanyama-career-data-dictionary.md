# Wembanyama career table: dictionary and provenance

`wembanyama-career-2023-2026.csv` joins Victor Wembanyama's three NBA regular seasons from five unmodified Basketball Reference exports in `assets/data/raw`:

- `WembyAdv.csv`: advanced rates and box-score estimates.
- `WembyP100.csv`: statistics per 100 possessions. `p100_individual_ortg` and `p100_individual_drtg` are individual Basketball Reference estimates; their difference is **not** used as on-court net rating.
- `WembyAdjustedShooting.csv`: shooting fields, league-relative `+` indices, and added shooting measures. An index of 100 is league average; for example TS+ 108 means 108% of league-average true-shooting efficiency, not eight percentage points above average.
- `WembyShooting.csv`: shot-distance attempt shares, accuracy by distance, assisted share of made 2P/3P, dunks, corner threes, and half-court heaves.
- `WembyPlayByPlay.csv`: estimated position shares, on-court and on/off net ratings per 100 possessions, turnover/foul event counts, and miscellaneous play-by-play counts.

## Duplicate grouped headers

The preparation script reads grouped exports with `csv.reader` and maps by asserted column index plus both header rows. It never constructs a dictionary from duplicate leaf headings.

- Shooting `% of` columns 10–15 are attempt shares: aggregate 2P, four mutually exclusive 2P distance bins, and 3P. The chart stacks only the four bins plus 3P, never aggregate 2P.
- Shooting `FG%` columns 16–21 are accuracy for aggregate 2P, the four distance bins, and 3P.
- Shooting `% of` columns 22–23 are the assisted shares of **made** 2P and made 3P. They are not attempt shares or shooting percentages.
- `Dunk/%FGA` is dunk attempts as a share of all FGA; `Dunk/#` is dunks made. `Corn/%3PA` and `Corn/3P%` describe corner threes. `1/2/Att.` and `1/2/Md.` are half-court heave attempts and makes.
- Play-by-play `+/−/OnCourt` and `+/−/On-Off` are net-rating measures per 100 possessions, not cumulative plus/minus.
- The first `Foul/Shoot` and `Foul/Off.` pair is fouls committed. The second pair is fouls drawn.
- `Misc/PGA` is points generated by assists; `And1` is made field goals resulting in an additional free throw; `Blkd` is field-goal attempts blocked.

## Naming and units

- `adv_*` preserves Advanced-table source values.
- `p100_*` preserves Per 100 Possessions source values.
- `adjusted_source_*`, `league_relative_*`, and `adjusted_*_added` preserve Adjusted Shooting values.
- `shooting_attempt_share_*`, `shooting_accuracy_*`, and `shooting_assisted_share_*` are source decimal shares (`.293` = 29.3%).
- `play_*` preserves Play-by-Play source values. Position fields are percentage points; ratings are points per 100 possessions; turnover, foul, and miscellaneous fields are season counts.
- `derived_unassisted_share_made_two_p` and `derived_unassisted_share_made_three_p` equal `1 − assisted share`.
- `derived_shot_zone_attempt_share_total` sums the four mutually exclusive 2P distance bins plus 3P.

## Validation and rounding

The join rejects missing/duplicate seasons, header drift, non-finite values, identity conflicts, and disagreements among overlapping TS%, FTr, 3PAr, FG%, 2P%, 3P%, eFG%, and FT% fields. Age, games, games started, and minutes match across all five files.

Source shot shares are rounded to three decimals. Their displayed sums are 100.0%, 100.1%, and 100.0%; the chart does not normalize them and uses a shared 100.1% plotting domain. Derived unassisted shares inherit the source's rounding. The displayed rookie-to-third-year changes, +8.1 percentage points for unassisted made twos and +9.6 points for assisted made threes, are differences between rounded source shares.
