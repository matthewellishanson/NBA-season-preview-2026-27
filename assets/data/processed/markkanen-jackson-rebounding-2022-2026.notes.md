# Markkanen and Jackson rebounding chart data

Generated by `scripts/prepare_jazz_charts.py` from the supplied Advanced and Per 100 Possessions regular-season tables for Lauri Markkanen and Jaren Jackson Jr., plus Markkanen's valid Per Game table.

The supplied `JJJ_PerGame.csv` is excluded. Its bytes and SHA-256 digest are identical to `MarkkanenAdv.csv`, and its schema/content are Markkanen advanced statistics rather than Jackson per-game statistics. The raw file is preserved unchanged. Jackson's games and total minutes come from his matching Advanced and Per 100 tables, so the chart does not need that invalid file.

Rows are joined only when season, team/aggregate identifier, age, league, position, games and starts agree. Coverage is 2022-23 through 2025-26. Aggregate `2TM`/`TOT` rows are preferred over component stints if both are present. Jackson's 2025-26 `2TM` row is labeled Memphis/Utah combined. Percentages retain percentage units; per-100 offensive, defensive and total rebounds are retained for auditability but not charted.
