# Miami Heat preview chart preparation

- Generated by `scripts/prepare_heat_preview.py` from the supplied Basketball-Reference exports in `assets/data/raw`; raw files are unchanged.
- Career rows are keyed by season after validating one non-aggregate team row per season. Miami tables are joined by stable `Player-additional` Basketball-Reference IDs.
- Grouped shooting and adjusted-shooting headers are combined with their group labels so duplicate fields such as `2P`, `3P`, `0-3` and assisted-share columns remain distinct. `% of FG Ast'd` is not used as an attempt measure.
- Per-game `MP` is kept as MPG; advanced/per-100 `MP` is total minutes. Shooting and attempt shares are stored as fractions; advanced `USG%` remains in percentage units. Missing values and Klay's 2019-20/2020-21 absences remain blank, not zero. Lines must not bridge those absences.
- Completed seasons are separated from the five-game 2026-27 snapshot. The current Miami per-game team export is not supplied, but the Bam and Klay career exports contain their five-game rows.
- Giannis has 157 minutes in `MIA_P100P.csv` and 158 on-court minutes in `MIA_OnOff.csv`; both source values are preserved and the discrepancy is not reconciled by invention. `MIA_Lineups.csv` contains team-minus-opponent differentials, including shooting percentages, and is not used as lineup shooting accuracy.
- The five departures logged 7,520 of 19,707 recorded Miami player-minutes in `MIA_Adv_2526.csv` (38.16%, described as approximately 38%). This is rotation usage, not lost wins or forecast replacement minutes. Ware's source row is 9.0 rebounds in 22.1 MPG.
- No supplied completed-season 2025-26 PerGame, P100P and Adv tables contain Bobby Portis or Tim Hardaway Jr. Their five-game Miami rows are not substituted. Those three 2025-26 table types (with stable player IDs) are required to add each player to the matrix.
- The source lineup and on/off tables cover five Miami games; they do not supply complete possession-level both/only/neither splits and are not used to claim long-term pair fit.
- League-relative shooting indices use 100 as league average. No exact league-average percentage is reverse-engineered from rounded indices. Exact 2025-26 three-point makes and attempts are not present in the matrix inputs, so none are reconstructed from rounded per-game or per-100 values.
