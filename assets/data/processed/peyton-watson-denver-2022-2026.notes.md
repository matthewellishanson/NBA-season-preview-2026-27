# Peyton Watson chart data

`peyton-watson-denver-2022-2026.csv` is generated by
`scripts/prepare_watson_cavaliers.py` from the five preserved source files in
`assets/data/raw/`.

The script excludes non-season summary rows, requires one unique row for each
regular season from 2022-23 through 2025-26, and joins the Per Game, Per 100
Possessions, Advanced and On-Off tables on season, age, team, league, position,
games and starts. It also validates every prefixed field in the supplied merged
file against the four original tables before writing chart-ready data.

Unit handling follows the source definitions: `minutes_per_game` comes from
Per Game `MP`; `total_minutes` comes from the matching Advanced, Per 100 and
On-Off `MP` fields; `points_per_100` comes from Per 100 `PTS`; `ts_pct` remains
a fraction; and `usage_pct` remains in percentage units. Blank source values
remain blank. The two observed on/off fields are retained for future use but
are not charted in this release.

These data describe Peyton Watson's Denver regular-season results. They do not
isolate games played without Aaron Gordon and do not project Watson's impact in
Cleveland.
