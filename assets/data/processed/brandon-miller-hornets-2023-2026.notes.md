# Brandon Miller career-development chart data

`brandon-miller-hornets-2023-2026.csv` is generated by
`scripts/prepare_miller_hornets.py` from four preserved Basketball Reference
exports in `assets/data/raw/`: `Miller_PerGame.csv`, `Miller_P100P.csv`,
`Miller_Adv.csv` and `Miller_AdjShooting.csv`.

The script inspects the two-row grouped header in the adjusted-shooting export,
requires one unique Charlotte (`CHO`) row per season in every table, then joins
by season and team. It also verifies age, league, position, games and starts,
and requires total minutes to match across the per-100, advanced and adjusted
shooting tables.

`minutes_per_game` comes only from the per-game `MP` field. `total_minutes`
comes from the advanced `MP` field. `points_per_100` comes from per-100 `PTS`.
Advanced `TS%` remains a fraction in the chart-ready CSV and is multiplied by
100 only when displayed. `USG%` remains in percentage units. Adjusted-shooting
`TS+` is a league-relative index where 100 is league average, not a
percentage-point difference.

The play-by-play and shooting-location exports are not needed for this chart.
These season-level results do not isolate Miller's minutes without LaMelo Ball,
establish causation between usage and efficiency, or project a larger role.
